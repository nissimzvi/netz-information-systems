Accessibility Statement

Information about website accessibility features and how to contact us about accessibility.

Our commitment

We work to make this website clear and usable for people with disabilities and continue improving accessibility as part of ongoing website maintenance.

Accessibility features

  • Clear heading and content hierarchy.
  • Keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images.
  • Responsive layout and text enlargement support.
  • A skip-to-content link.
  • A compact accessibility control for text size, contrast and link emphasis.
